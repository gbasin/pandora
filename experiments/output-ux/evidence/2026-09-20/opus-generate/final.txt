## How it was produced

`pnpm generate` runs the generator on a remote host and, by design, does **not** write to the workspace. It exits 75 and drops the generated file plus a unified diff under `.pandora-artifacts/<run-id>/` (gitignored), with a message telling you to apply them yourself. I reviewed the diff, applied it, and confirmed the workspace file is byte-identical to the generated artifact (`diff` clean).
