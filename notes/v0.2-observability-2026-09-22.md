## Found and fixed

* **The daemon's `stats` op raised on every call.** It called `stats(since=...)`
  against `def stats(self, window)`. Now tested over the socket.
* **The drift rule fired on `warn`.** Under `warn` the verdict stands and the
  run's own notice already says so. The rule is now `drift = "fail"` only.
* **The gitignored rule never saw the snapshot.** `Submission.shipped` now
  carries the frozen manifest's paths, so a `[sync] include`d path is not blamed.
* **Unclaimed heavy commands were never logged.** The assembly lost the POC's
  `heavy` path: the shim ignored the key and `enrol` wrote none. Restored.
  `DEFAULT_HEAVY` is build, lint, typecheck, install, i, dev, test, exec, less any
  claimed form. **eichler must be re-enrolled** for its marker to gain the
  lines; until then `local, not routed` reads 0.
* **`test_a_peak_is_observed_for_the_group` failed on a busy Mac.** macOS
  compressed or paged out the child's idle 120 MiB within a second, and the
  sampler read 10-52 MiB. The child now keeps its pages touched.
